# 02. Product Requirements (PRD)

## Problem statement
Hotel teams currently use fragmented tools for website CMS, bookings, rates, and operations. HarborOS unifies these workflows.

## Goals
1. Deliver workflow-first admin for non-technical teams.
2. Support web, app, and partner APIs from one source of truth.
3. Enable modular deployment as framework/packages.

## Non-goals
- Building a full ERP.
- Replacing every existing PMS from day one.

## Functional requirements
- FR-1: Property and room setup.
- FR-2: Availability and rate management.
- FR-3: Reservation lifecycle and payment status.
- FR-4: Housekeeping and maintenance tasks.
- FR-5: Website content management.
- FR-6: Reporting and export.
- FR-7: Connector framework.

Mapped in [Traceability Matrix](./traceability-matrix.md).
